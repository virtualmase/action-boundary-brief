# Action Boundary Brief Showcase — Design Direction

## Three possible approaches

| Theme name | Very brief intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| **Work Permit** | A technical field permit with slots, stamps, and formal check rows. The voice would be concrete but risks looking like a compliance form. | 0.06 |
| **Decision Relay** | A light, kinetic route map that visualizes an action moving only when its boundary facts are named. It frames the skill as a shared handoff rather than a checklist. | 0.08 |
| **Quiet Ledger** | An archival document surface with structured specimen notes. It gives the method a studied, minimal quality. | 0.02 |

## Chosen approach: Decision Relay

**Design movement.** Technical wayfinding and operational control-room notation, translated into a calm, accessible web document.

**Core principles.** Make the action boundary visible before explaining it. Use a single five-stage relay rather than repeated card grids. Keep every claim qualified by a method limit. Treat the showcase as a document a reviewer can read, not a funnel a visitor must traverse.

**Color philosophy.** Warm paper supports long reading; carbon text gives operational density; cobalt marks a route that has a named boundary; rust identifies a pause or unknown. Colors represent state, never a persuasion tactic.

**Layout paradigm.** An offset left evidence rail anchors a wide relay field. The page progresses through a horizontally connected sequence on large screens and becomes a vertical decision path on narrow screens.

**Signature elements.** Five connected relay gates, small square field markers, and a specimen panel that changes only between prewritten fictional cases.

**Interaction philosophy.** A visitor may select a fictional action type to reveal the questions the skill would surface. The page does not offer a score, collect a response, or tell a visitor their action is safe.

**Animation.** Cobalt route segments enter with a short opacity and transform transition. Hover states move only the arrow marker. All animation respects reduced motion.

**Typography system.** Decision voice: Georgia. Working language: Arial and Helvetica. Trace labels: SFMono-Regular, Consolas, and Liberation Mono. Use expansive serif questions and compact mono state labels.

**Brand essence.** Action Boundary Brief is a public pre-action skill for people who need to make AI-enabled work inspectable before access becomes authority. **Practical, restrained, accountable.**

**Brand voice.** State what the method does and what it refuses to do. Example lines: “Name the limit before the action crosses it.” and “A completed brief is a starting record, not a permission slip.”

**Wordmark and logo.** A five-segment relay mark: a horizontal route interrupted by five gates, with the third gate open only to indicate a named decision right. Use the mark with an uppercase condensed-style wordmark.

**Signature brand color.** **Relay Cobalt — `#1746C8`**.
